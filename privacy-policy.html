DesiRiders app: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026 (version 1.2.0)

This policy explains how the DesiRiders app ("the app") handles information. DesiRiders is a cycling club; contact us via desiriders.cc/contact.html or @desiridersuk.

What the app collects

Contact form. If you use the contact form, the name, email address, subject and message you type are sent to the club through our form provider (Formspree) so we can reply. We use them only to respond to you.

On your phone. The app stores, on your device only, the rider profile you choose with "This is me", your best game scores, and a saved copy of the club ride data. This is not sent to us.

Rider accounts. If you sign in under "My rides", we use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to handle it. We store your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the name on your Google account. We store the trip details the club exec add for you, such as your flights, room, bike hire and kit size, so we can show them to you. You must verify your email address, and other riders cannot see your personal details. Only the club exec can view and edit them.

What the app does not do

The app has no advertising, no analytics and no location tracking.

Information from our website

The app shows club information loaded from desiriders.cc, including rider names, photos and rides taken part in, as already published on our website. Kit photos are loaded from Google Drive. Links open other sites (for example WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube and our sponsors), which have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your rider account at any time inside the app: open My rides, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account. This removes your login and the trip details we hold for you (such as flights and room details). We keep your account and trip details until you delete them or ask us to.

If you can't use the app, you can ask us to delete your account and data by messaging us through desiriders.cc/contact.html or @desiridersuk, using the email address you signed in with. See also how to delete your account.

Your choices

You can clear the information stored on your phone by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. To have your rider account and trip details deleted, contact us and we will remove them. To ask us to change or remove your details from the club's rider list or to delete a contact-form message, contact us using the details above.

Children

The app is a general club app and is not aimed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
