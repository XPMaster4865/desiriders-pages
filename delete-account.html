Delete your DesiRiders app account

In the app

  1. Open the DesiRiders app and go to My rides.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

This permanently removes your login, your profile (name, phone number and notification codes), the trip details we hold for you (such as flights, booking references and room details), your ride requests and your live map position. It can't be undone. SOS alerts you've raised stay in the club's SOS log for up to 30 days, then are deleted automatically. If the app asks you to sign in again first, do that and repeat the steps.

If you can't use the app

Message us through desiriders.cc/contact.html or @desiridersuk, using the email address you signed in with, and ask us to delete your account and data.

Details of what we store are in our privacy policy.
